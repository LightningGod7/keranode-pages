Chandler

A personal assistant that organises one person's own Google Drive.

Chandler is a private tool built and run by its owner for a single Google account. It reads and files documents in that account's Drive, such as bank statements and receipts, so they land in the right folder without manual sorting.

It is not a product, there are no other users, and no account may sign in to it besides the owner's own.
