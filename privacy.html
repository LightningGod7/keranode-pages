Privacy policy

Chandler. Last updated 7 October 2026.

Chandler is a personal tool with exactly one user, its owner. It is not offered to anyone else and collects nothing from anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, Chandler uses the Google Drive API to read, create, move and organise files in the owner's own Google Drive. It accesses no other Google service and no other account.

Where data goes

Files stay between Google and the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any third party, nothing is sold, and nothing is shared. There is no analytics and no tracking on this site.

Storage of credentials

The access token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own machine, readable by the owner alone. It is never transmitted anywhere else.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Chandler's access at any time from the Google account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking it stops all access immediately.

Retention

Chandler keeps no copy of Drive contents beyond what the owner already has in their own Drive and on their own machine.
